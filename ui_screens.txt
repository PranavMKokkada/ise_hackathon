### 1️⃣ Global Dashboard – “BioNexus Command Center”

**Prompt:**
Design a **1440px wide desktop Global Dashboard** for an enterprise bio-surveillance platform called **BioNexus**. The aesthetic is **Futuristic Enterprise SaaS, dark mode**, inspired by Palantir/SpaceX mission control: deep navy background, neon cyan accents, subtle grid lines, and clean data visualization.

**Overall Layout & Structure**

* **Viewport:** 1440px width desktop, no mobile layout in this screen.
* **Top App Bar (64px height):** Full-width, sticky.

  * Left: Product logo “BioNexus” (wordmark) + small “Command Center” label.
  * Center: Global region selector (pill dropdown with flag/icon + text) and date/time indicator.
  * Right: Icons for notifications (badge count), WebSocket connection status (dot), user avatar with name/role dropdown.
* **Main Layout:** Two-column structure over a dark background.

  * **Left (approx 70% width): Hero 3D Globe Panel.**
  * **Right (approx 30% width): Side Panel with stacked cards** (Critical Alerts, Global Stats, Quick Actions).
* **Bottom Dock:** Full-width **Timeline & Layer Toggle** strip.

**Hero Panel – 3D Globe & Map Layers**

* **Card style:** Large, edge-to-edge within left area, 24px padding, 20–24px border radius.
* Background: slightly lighter dark (#050B1A) with subtle radial gradient.
* Center: **3D globe** (placeholder illustration) with arcs and glowing hotspots.
* Top-left of globe card: “Global Outbreak Map” title + mini legend (Outbreak risk, Environmental factors, Supply stress).
* Top-right: **Layer toggle chips**:

  * “Outbreak Risk”, “Environmental Drivers”, “Supply Chain Fragility”, “Mobility Flows”, “Shortages”.
  * Each chip: pill with neon border when active; inactive chips use muted border.
* On hover over hotspots: show small tooltip card with region name, risk score, outbreak status.

**Right Side Panel – Alerts & Stats**

* **Critical Alerts Feed (top card):**

  * Title row: “Critical Alerts” + severity filter pill (All / High / Medium).
  * Scrollable list of alert rows (auto-scroll vertical). Each row: alert icon, region, severity tag (red/amber badge), short description, timestamp.
  * Hover: row lifts slightly with subtle glow, click opens details drawer (right slide-over, not separate screen).
* **Global Statistics (middle card):**

  * Four KPI tiles in 2x2 grid: “Active Outbreaks”, “Regions at High Risk”, “Critical Shortages”, “Active Supply Disruptions”.
  * Each tile: large number, tiny sparkline, trend chip (▲12% / ▼5%).
* **Quick Actions (bottom card):**

  * Three large buttons: “Run Simulation”, “View 21-Day Predictions”, “Hospital Mode”.

**Timeline & Interaction Dock (Bottom Strip)**

* Full-width card dock (80–100px height) anchored to bottom of main area.
* Left: “Time Horizon” label + play/pause button for timeline animation.
* Center: **Timeline slider** 0–21 days with labeled ticks at 0, 7, 14, 21.
* Right: active date label + small toggle “Auto-play”.

**Colour Palette & Theme**

* Background: #020617
* Surfaces: #050B1A / #0B1220
* Primary Accent: #38BDF8 (cyan)
* Secondary Accent: #4ADE80 (green)
* Warning: #FACC15, Danger: #FB7185
* Text primary: #F9FAFB, secondary: #9CA3AF, subtle labels: #6B7280

**Typography**

* Font: **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
* Page title: 24–28px, semibold. Card titles: 16–18px, semibold. Body text: 14px regular. KPI numbers: 28–32px medium.

**Buttons & Components**

* Buttons: medium size, 10px radius, semi-solid with gradient from #0EA5E9 to #22D3EE.
* Hover: outer glow, slight scale-up (1.02), text turns pure white.
* Icons: line icons with neon stroke.

**Interactions & Animations**

* Globe: slow idle rotation; on drag, rotate with easing. Hovering hotspots pulses them gently.
* Timeline: dragging thumb updates subtle “rewind/fast-forward” motion on globe arcs.
* Cards: on mount, fade-in + slide-up 8px using **Framer Motion style** easing.
* Alerts feed: auto-scroll with smooth easing; new alerts briefly highlight with a cyan border.

**Constraints**

* **No left nav drawer.** Navigation is entirely via the top bar and in-content buttons.
* Screen must feel like a **mission control center** for outbreak + supply + risk.
* Layout must be 1440px desktop-first, responsive down only to 1280px without collapsing major regions.

### 2️⃣ Region Drill-Down – “Regional Risk Intelligence View”

**Prompt:**
Design a **desktop Region Drill-Down screen** for a single region (e.g., “Maharashtra, India”) in the same **Futuristic Enterprise SaaS dark theme** as the Global Dashboard. This screen zooms in on one region’s outbreaks, inventory, and recommendations.

**Layout & Structure**

* **Top Bar (64px, reused from system):**

  * Left: Back button (“← Global Dashboard”), region breadcrumb (“Global › India › Maharashtra”).
  * Center: Region name + colored risk badge (“High”, “Medium”, “Low”).
  * Right: Last updated timestamp, refresh icon, user avatar.
* **Main Content:** 3-column layout with a central focus.

  * **Top row:** Full-width **RegionHeader** summary strip.
  * **Middle row:** 3 cards in 3-column layout: Outbreak Predictions, Inventory Status, Environmental Factors.
  * **Bottom row:** Left = LocalMapView (wide). Right = ActionRecommendations card stacked above Download/Share controls.

**RegionHeader Strip**

* Horizontal summary card (full width, 120px height).
* Left: Region name, subtext (“Population 15.2M, Urban-dominant”), risk score chip (numeric 0–100 with circular mini gauge).
* Center: Horizontal risk bar (gradient from green to red) with marker for current level and label (“Risk Level: High – Escalating”).
* Right: Two compact KPI pills: “Next 10 days cases +18%” and “Critical items: 5 at risk”; a small “Share” icon button.

**OutbreakPredictionsCard**

* Title: “Outbreak Predictions – Next 10 Days”.
* List of diseases as rows: each row shows disease name, small line chart, % change, confidence tag.
* Top-right of card: dropdown for forecast horizon (7 / 10 / 21 days).
* Hover on row: highlight row and show tooltip with detailed numbers.

**InventoryStatusCard**

* Title: “Inventory Status – Critical Medical Items”.
* Table-like layout with columns: Item, Days Remaining, Stock Level bar, Status tag.
* At top: donut gauge showing “Overall Capacity %”.
* Low-stock rows use warning (amber) or danger (red) tags.

**EnvironmentalFactorsCard**

* Title: “Environmental Drivers”.
* Three small line/area charts stacked or in grid: Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall.
* Below charts: mini map thumbnail showing mosquito breeding zones as glowing red patches.

**LocalMapView**

* 2D regional map (Leaflet-like) card with 20px radius.
* Shows hospitals (cross icon markers), supply nodes (factory/warehouse icons), and shortage icons (red exclamation).
* Top of map card: small filter row (checkboxes for Hospitals, Supply Nodes, Shortages).
* Hover on marker: tooltip with label; click: small right-floating info bubble with more details.

**ActionRecommendationsCard**

* Title: “Recommended Actions – Next 14 Days”.
* Numbered list with top 3 actions:

  * Each item shows an icon (stockpile, routing, governance), short title, micro-description, priority tag (P1/P2).
* Bottom area: secondary list titled “Government Notices” with pill tags for categories (Policy, Logistics, Public Advisory).
* Right side of card footer: **Primary button “Download Region Report”** and ghost button “Copy Link”.

**Colour, Typography & Components**

* Same palette and font as Global Dashboard.
* Maintain 24px spacing between cards, consistent 16–20px padding inside cards.
* Card headers have cyan accent line or subtle top border.

**Interactions & Animations**

* Region risk badge animates (slight pulse) if risk is “High” and data updated in last few minutes.
* Download Region Report: show non-blocking toast “Report generating…” followed by “Report ready” with link.
* Hover on map markers and disease rows uses soft cyan glow.

**Constraints**

* Desktop-only screen, 1440px target.
* No modal-heavy UX; most interactions stay in-page (toasts, small drawers allowed).
* Must clearly communicate **risk + inventory + environment** at a glance.

### 3️⃣ Simulation Mode – “What-If Scenario Lab”

**Prompt:**
Design a **Simulation Mode desktop screen** in the same **dark, futuristic enterprise** theme. This page focuses on configuring and comparing simulation scenarios.

**Layout & Structure**

* **Top Bar:**

  * Left: Title “Simulation Mode” + small label “What-if Scenario Lab”.
  * Right: Scenario dropdown (Saved simulations), “New Scenario” button, user avatar.
* **Main Split Layout:**

  * Left side (35% width): **SimulationControlPanel** (sticky vertical panel).
  * Right side (65% width): **ComparisonView** and **ImpactSummary** stacked vertically.
* **Bottom area:** TimelineComparison card spanning full width.

**SimulationControlPanel**

* Card with vertical step-like layout.
* Section 1: Scenario selection.

  * Dropdown for “Predefined Scenario” with tags (Flood, Pandemic Wave, Export Ban).
  * Small description text under selected scenario.
* Section 2: Parameter sliders (rainfall change, mobility variation, infection multiplier, supply severity).

  * Each slider:

    * Label, current numeric value chip, min/max labels.
    * Style: neon cyan track, thumb with subtle glow, track background muted.
* Section 3: Buttons row.

  * Primary: “Run Simulation” (prominent).
  * Secondary: “Reset Parameters” ghost button.
* At bottom: info box showing “Est. runtime: ~X seconds” and last simulation ID.

**ComparisonView**

* Title: “Current vs Simulated State”.
* Two side-by-side cards:

  * Left card: “Current State” – mini map or globe snapshot plus KPIs.
  * Right card: “Simulated State” – similar layout but with **diff overlays**.
* Each card shows:

  * Region scope selector (Global / Region / Custom).
  * Outbreak heatmap thumbnail.
  * 3–4 KPIs: Predicted Cases, Shortage Regions, Critical Alerts, Supply Stress.
* Between them: column of “Change indicators” with ▲/▼ percentages and arrows.

**ImpactSummaryCard**

* Title: “Impact Summary”.
* Two-column layout:

  * Left: textual bullets: “New hotspots: X”, “Additional shortages: Y regions”, “Population affected: Z M”.
  * Right: small horizontal bar chart stacked by severity levels.
* Footer: “Recommended Actions” pill list (e.g., “Redistribute PPE”, “Activate Alternative Supplier Network”).

**TimelineComparison**

* Full-width card at bottom.
* Dual-line chart: current vs simulated metrics (e.g., cases, shortages) over time.
* Legend: “Current” (muted cyan), “Simulated” (brighter cyan/green).
* Top-right: “Export Simulation Report” button.

**Colours, Typography & Components**

* Same global palette.
* Simulation sections may use purple accent (#A855F7) subtly to differentiate from real-time views.
* Font: Inter/SF Pro; bigger emphasis on numeric data.

**Interactions & Animations**

* Run Simulation:

  * Button enters loading state: spinner inside, label changes to “Running…”.
  * Control panel dims slightly; right-side shows skeleton loaders.
  * On completion, content fades in with slight slide.
* Sliders: smooth update, numerical chip counts up/down with small easing.
* Export Simulation Report: shows toast and subtle progress indicator at top-right.

**Constraints**

* Desktop-first, 1440px.
* No full-screen modal takeover. Results must remain visible alongside controls for iteration.
* Screen should convey **experimentation and safety**: changes are simulated, not live.

### 5️⃣ Supply Chain Mapping View – “Network Intelligence Map”

**Prompt:**
Design a **Supply Chain Mapping View** screen for visualizing deep-tier supply networks in the same dark futuristic enterprise style.

**Layout & Structure**

* **Top Bar:**

  * Left: “Supply Chain Network” title.
  * Center: disease filter dropdown (“Show network for: [Disease]”).
  * Right: buttons “Find Alternative Suppliers” and “Export Recommendations” (disabled until a node is selected).
* **Main Content Layout:**

  * Left: large **SupplyChainGraphView** (network diagram).
  * Right: vertical stack of **Node Details Panel**, **FilterPanel**, and **DisruptionTimelineCard**.
* **Bottom Overlay:** GlobalMapOverlay as a collapsible panel.

**SupplyChainGraphView**

* Centerpiece with dark canvas background, faint geometric grid.
* Nodes:

  * Different shapes for Disease, Treatment, Active Ingredient, Factory, Raw Material, Region, Supplier.
  * Colors indicate risk/fragility level: green → amber → red.
* Edges:

  * Thin curved lines; thickness indicates volume or importance.
  * Disrupted edges are dashed/glowing red.
* Top-left mini legend for node types and risk colors.
* Top-right controls for zoom, fit-to-screen, and layout mode (force-directed / layered tiers).

**Node Details Panel**

* Docked panel on right, 320–360px wide.
* When no node is selected: show placeholder with hint “Select a node in the graph to see details.”
* When node selected:

  * Header: node name + type badge + risk score chip.
  * Sections:

    * “Status & Risk”: textual summary, status tag.
    * “Dependencies”: pill list of connected nodes (“Supplies X”, “Depends on Y”).
    * “Recent Events”: mini list with event icons (strike, flood, export ban), date, severity.
  * “Find Alternatives” button anchored at bottom (primary).

**FilterPanel**

* Card below Node Details (or collapsible side section).
* Controls:

  * Multi-select dropdown for disease type.
  * Region multi-select.
  * Checkboxes: “Show only critical paths”, “Highlight disrupted nodes”.
* Apply/Reset buttons at bottom; filters apply with animated transitions on graph (nodes fade in/out).

**DisruptionTimelineCard**

* Small card listing current and upcoming disruptions as a vertical timeline.
* Each event: icon, label, affected node, date range, severity indicator.

**GlobalMapOverlay**

* Collapsible bottom panel (~30% height) with a world map.
* Shows factories, ports, regions, and disruption markers as icons.
* Toggle button to collapse/expand; map uses same dark theme with glowing outlines.

**Visuals & Interactions**

* Clicking node highlights its neighbors; others fade.
* Hover shows quick tooltip with name and risk.
* Panning & zooming on the graph must feel smooth, with slight inertia.
* When “Find Alternatives” is clicked:

  * Node Details shows loading shimmer, then a list of alternative suppliers with capacity bars and lead time badges. Buttons “Export as CSV”.

**Constraints**

* Desktop, 1440px.
* No draggable windows; keep a clean main canvas + right panel pattern.
* Focus on **clarity of complex graph**, not ornamental visuals.

  * First row: MetricsGrid (4 KPI cards).
  * Second row: two-column: PredictionAccuracyChart (left) and SupplyChainHealthScore (right).

**MetricsGrid**

* Four equal cards in a 2x2 grid.
* Cards:

**PredictionAccuracyChart**

**SupplyChainHealthScore**

* Right card with gauge plus breakdown list.
* Gauge: semi-circular, color-coded.
* Below: list of contributing factors with small bar indicators (e.g., Disruptions, Inventory, Lead Times).

**RegionalPerformanceHeatmap**

* World/regional map card with heat tiles or choropleth.
* On click on a region: right-aligned sidebar slides open to show region-focused metrics.

**ExportSection**

* Compact card with three primary actions:

  * “Export Full PDF Report”, “Export CSV Data”, “Open API Docs”.

* Card with form-like layout:

  * Multi-select for metrics (checkboxes for predictions, disruptions, inventory, etc.).

  * Region filters (global/selected).
* Primary button “Generate Custom Report”.
* Show subtle preview hint: “This will generate a downloadable PDF/CSV”.

**Style & Interactions**

* Same dark palette; use more cyan and green for “accuracy/success” feel.
* Charts animate on load (lines draw in, bars grow).
* Export actions show toasts for “Report generation started” and “Report ready”.
* Clicking API Docs opens new tab (just visually represented).

**Constraints**

* Desktop, 1440px.
* DO NOT use popups for exports; keep everything inline and toast-based.

**HyperLocalWarningsCard**
